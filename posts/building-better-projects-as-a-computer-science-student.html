Building Better Projects as a Computer Science Student

Fewer projects, more finished thinking.

By Prathamesh Birelliwar · Jul 28, 2026 · 8 min read

Every student portfolio has a to-do app, a weather app, and a calculator. None of them are bad, but none of them say anything about you.

Start from a real annoyance

The best projects solve a problem you personally have. You know the users, you know when it breaks, and you will care enough to finish it.

Document the decisions

A short write-up about why you chose one approach over another is worth more than another feature. It shows how you think, which is what people hire for.

Depth beats breadth when someone has two minutes to look.

Pick one project this semester. Deploy it, write about it, and let a stranger use it.
