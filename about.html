ABOUT

Behind the ideas.

Who I am

I am Prathamesh Birelliwar, an Artificial Intelligence and Data Science student with a curiosity for understanding how technology works and a habit of learning by building. I enjoy exploring programming, artificial intelligence, machine learning, data science, and web development while continuously improving the way I approach technical problems.

The Build Journal is a place where I document that journey. Instead of keeping everything I learn inside notebooks or scattered across different projects, I use this space to turn those experiences into articles, explanations, and practical notes that can also be useful to someone else learning the same things.

What I write about

Most of my writing revolves around programming, artificial intelligence, machine learning, data science, web development, and software projects. Some articles focus on technical concepts and try to explain them in a simple and practical way, while others come from lessons learned while actually building something.

I am particularly interested in the space between learning something and applying it. A concept can make perfect sense while studying it, but building a real project often reveals the questions that tutorials do not answer. This blog is where I try to explore that gap and document what I discover.

My interests

My interests currently revolve around artificial intelligence, machine learning, programming, data, and building useful software. I also enjoy learning about how different technologies connect with one another and how a simple idea can gradually become a working project.

Beyond individual technologies, I am interested in the process of learning itself — understanding difficult concepts, solving problems, making mistakes, debugging them, and eventually turning those experiences into something useful.

Technologies I work with

My current technical toolkit includes Python, C++, C, SQL, HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. I also work with tools and libraries such as NumPy, Pandas, Matplotlib, Git, GitHub, Jupyter Notebook, and MySQL.

As I continue learning Artificial Intelligence and Data Science, I am also exploring machine learning concepts, data analysis, algorithms, databases, and the different technologies required to turn an idea into a complete project.

My journey

My journey in technology is still in its early stages, and that is exactly what makes it interesting. Rather than pretending to have everything figured out, I want this timeline to represent the process of learning, experimenting, building projects, and gradually becoming a better developer.

  2. 2025

  3. 2026

  4. 2026
